## Content Quality Findings

### Overall Content Profile
- **Business type:** Travel / informational content site about Chinese mountain destinations.
- Pages break into three templates: mountain detail (25 pages), guides (6 pages), blog posts (3 pages), plus listing/index pages.

### Thin Content
- **[/about/](D:\workspaces\website\chinese-mountains\dist\about\index.html):** Only two paragraphs (~200 words total). A thin "About" page hurts E-E-A-T signals. Expand to include team info, editorial standards, and sources.
- Blog listing page and guide listing pages are short but acceptable as index pages.

### Deep Content (Strong)
- Blog posts average 1,000-1,500 words with practical, original advice. The "First-Timer's Guide" is particularly strong.
- Mountain detail pages each include: description, cultural significance, highlights list, best season, how to get there, and FAQs. Consistent depth across all 25 mountain pages.
- Guide pages are well-structured with seasonal/target-audience framing.

### Readability
- Excellent. Short paragraphs, clear headings, bulleted lists. Flesch-friendly sentence structure throughout.
- No jargon without explanation. "UNESCO 1990" and similar abbreviations are given with context.

### E-E-A-T Assessment
- **Experience:** Content demonstrates first-hand knowledge (e.g., specific trail names, which cable cars exist, admission tips).
- **Expertise:** Domain-specific knowledge is deep -- the site covers mountains that generic travel sites miss (Namcha Barwa, Mount Gongga).
- **Authority:** Weak. No author bios, no citations, no external references. "Chinese Mountains Team" is used as a byline with no individual attribution.
- **Trust:** No contact page, no privacy policy, no terms of service. No HTTPS indicator visible in source (depends on deployment).

### Duplicate Content Risk
- Mountain pages share an identical meta description pattern: "Everything about [Name]: height, trails, weather, photos, history, travel tips." This is a detection risk for programmatic content.
- "You May Also Like" sections on mountain pages provide contextual internal links, which is positive.

### Score: 78/100
